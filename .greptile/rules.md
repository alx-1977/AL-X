# AL/X constitutional review mandate

`LAWS_OF_ALX.md` is the sole canonical law text. Review the whole change against every law in that file and the accepted architecture, including semantic behaviour that deterministic gates may miss.

For every meaningful change, ask:

1. Who is deciding meaning, the next action, continuation, or completion?
2. Is AL/X reasoning dynamically, or is code prescribing a route or journey?
3. Could AL/X discover, retrieve, infer, calculate, or configure this instead of us encoding it?
4. If this is experimental, is it isolated, limited to scoped read-only production access, unable to expand its own authority, and kept out of production until separately reviewed and authorised by Friedl?

Pay particular attention to harmless-looking coordinators, resolvers, handlers, strategies, fallbacks, frontend actions, state machines, bundled tools, provider adapters, and tests. Review what they do, not what they are called.

For a probable breach, write **AL/X LAW VIOLATION — BLOCKING** and include:

- the applicable Law number;
- the exact file and code responsible;
- the authority or reasoning moved out of AL/X;
- the required architectural correction; and
- the approved exception ID, if one already exists.

Functional behaviour, passing tests, or author assurance does not override a Law. Do not create or infer exceptions. Greptile supplements deterministic `law-gates`; it does not replace them or become an AL/X reasoning authority.
