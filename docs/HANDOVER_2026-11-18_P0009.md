- **How she runs:** AL/X runs from a snapshot of the branch, not from the checkout.
  - The snapshot is at `.git/alx-runtime/<sha>`, made with `git archive HEAD`.
  - She's started by the scratchpad `start_branch.py`. Env sets the board agent to `codex_subscription`, `gpt-6.1-sol`, effort `medium`.
  - Current snapshot: 50ab9c8.
- **Restart procedure:**
  1. Ask Friedl first, every time.

- **Her messages** are in `.alx/runtime/conversations.sqlite3`, table conversation_turns. The board agent's calls are in `.alx/runtime/board-jobs/<job>.calls.jsonl`. Edit stdout, with the tool's reports, is in the copy's `edit-*.stdout`.
